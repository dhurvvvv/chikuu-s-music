Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

CHIKU'S PLAY ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices, how your information is handled, and our privacy architecture.

Core Privacy Guarantee: CHIKU'S PLAY does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or data brokers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your email address and an optional display name or username. Authentication is securely managed via Supabase Auth using industry-standard hashing and encryption.
  • User Library Data: Songs you like, playlists you create, and your following list are stored in your secure, private database record linked exclusively to your authenticated user identifier.
  • Listening History: Recently played tracks are logged in rate-limited intervals solely to populate your "Recently Played" shelf.

2. Personal Wallpapers & Local Storage Privacy

CHIKU'S PLAY offers a personal wallpaper customization feature that allows you to choose an image from your device's photo gallery:

  • 100% On-Device Storage: When you select a wallpaper photo, the image is compressed and stored exclusively in your local browser's IndexedDB database on your own device.
  • Zero Server Transmission: Your personal photo is never uploaded to our servers, Supabase, or any third-party cloud.
  • Total Control: You can remove your wallpaper at any time via the Settings menu, which immediately purges the local database record and revokes in-memory object URLs.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Authentication & Database: We use Supabase (hosted over HTTPS) for user session management and catalog data delivery.
  • Google OAuth: If you choose "Continue with Google", authentication is performed directly via Google OAuth 2.0. We only receive your verified email and primary display name.
  • Audio Content Delivery: Catalog audio assets are streamed directly via secure HTTPS.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may update your profile or delete playlists at any time within the application. If you wish to delete your account and all associated library data permanently, you may request deletion through account settings or administrator contact.

5. Independent Platform Disclaimer

CHIKU'S PLAY is an independent web application. Spotify is a registered trademark of Spotify AB. CHIKU'S PLAY is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Spotify AB or any other third-party music streaming corporation.
